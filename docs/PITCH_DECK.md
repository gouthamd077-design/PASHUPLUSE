## Slide 2: The Ground Reality (The Problem)

### Visual Layout
Three problem pillar cards with dark backgrounds, red accent warning badges, and photo thumbnails.

### Slide Content
1. **Critical Treatment Delay:** In rural Karnataka, finding an available veterinarian takes 24–48 hours. By then, acute conditions like bloat or hemosepticemia become fatal.
2. **Language & Literacy Divide:** 82% of smallholder farmers speak only Kannada. Existing portals demand tedious typing in English or Hindi, causing immediate abandonment.
3. **Treatment Drop-off & Lost History:** Paper prescriptions get lost. 45% of farmers discontinue antibiotics after day 2 when fever dips, causing antibiotic resistance and chronic relapse.
4. **Silent Disease Outbreaks:** Highly contagious viral outbreaks like Foot-and-Mouth Disease (FMD) sweep through contiguous villages weeks before district officials detect them.

### Speaker Script (45 Seconds)
> *"When a cow stops eating in a rural village, the farmer faces a maze: language barriers, scattered paper prescriptions, and an agonizing wait for a doctor. When they do get medicine, there’s no tracking of whether doses were finished. And worse, when contagious diseases strike, neighboring villages remain in the dark until their own herds collapse. The market has plenty of record-keeping tools, but zero connected clinical workflows."*

## Slide 3: The Solution — PASHU PULSE

### Visual Layout
Clean 4-step progressive pipeline diagram connecting the icons: Farmer $\rightarrow$ AI Triage $\rightarrow$ Verified Vet $\rightarrow$ Digital Care & Recovery.

### Slide Content
* **The Connected Healthcare Journey:**
  * **Report:** Farmers speak in Kannada or upload a quick photo.
  * **Triage:** AI structures the symptoms and flags risk indicators (clearly labeled preliminary).
  * **Connect:** Intelligent routing notifies nearby verified veterinarians for fast acceptance.
  * **Treat:** Doctor issues a standardized digital prescription with morning/evening dose schedules.
  * **Recover:** AI monitors follow-up photos, tracks medicine adherence, and closes the case upon documented recovery.
  * **Warn:** Automatically clusters village cases into the Village Early Warning Radar.

### Speaker Script (40 Seconds)
> *"Pashu Pulse solves this with an end-to-end connected ecosystem. A farmer speaks into their phone in Kannada. Our AI structures a preliminary triage report. A nearby verified veterinarian accepts the case, prescribes digital treatment, and monitors the animal’s recovery through scheduled photo check-ins and dose confirmations. And every report actively feeds our village outbreak radar."*

## Slide 7: Signature Deep Dive 2 — Village Disease Early Warning Radar

### Visual Layout
Geospatial map visualization of Mysuru and Mandya showing color-coded cluster pins (Amber for Suspected, Blue for Under Verification, Red for Confirmed) with the official demonstration banner.

### Slide Content
* **Community-Level Biosurveillance:**
  * Aggregates anonymized symptom reports across taluks (Nanjangud, Srirangapatna, Maddur, Pandavapura, Hunsur).
  * AI flags statistical clusters before clinical laboratory confirmation.
* **Tri-Stage Outbreak Lifecycle:**
  * `SUSPECTED:` 3+ correlated cases detected within 5 km.
  * `UNDER VERIFICATION:` Local Veterinary Officer assigned to inspect on-ground.
  * `CONFIRMED:` Verified by veterinary authorities; Kannada audio alerts broadcast to dairy societies within a 10 km radius.
* **Responsible AI:** Prominent banner identifies synthetic test data to avoid unnecessary public alarm.

### Speaker Script (45 Seconds)
> *"This is our lead differentiator: the Village Disease Radar. In livestock farming, diseases like FMD spread from farm to farm in days. When three farmers in Pandavapura report mouth sores and drooling, our algorithm flags a Suspected Cluster. The taluk veterinary officer is dispatched to verify it. Once confirmed, dairy co-ops and farmers in that radius receive instant Kannada advisories. We catch outbreaks weeks before traditional government paperwork."*

## Slide 8: Clinical Safety & Responsible AI Governance

### Visual Layout
Clean medical governance framework chart with clear green/red boundary badges.

### Slide Content
* **Strict Guardrails:**
  * **AI Never Diagnoses:** AI outputs are strictly formatted as *Preliminary Triage Summaries* to help prioritize cases.
  * **AI Never Prescribes:** Only licensed, verified veterinarians can prescribe medications or modify treatment plans.
  * **Emergency Bypass:** Pashu SOS requests bypass AI queues and route directly to human doctors immediately.
  * **Privacy by Design:** Village cluster maps expose village-level aggregates only—never farmer names, phone numbers, or exact coordinates.

### Speaker Script (35 Seconds)
> *"We hold uncompromising clinical and ethical standards. In animal health, an incorrect AI prescription can kill livestock and cause antibiotic resistance. That is why our AI strictly prepares preliminary case notes. Every diagnosis, every dosage, and every prescription is issued by a verified veterinarian with a registered council number."*

## Slide 11: Technical Architecture & Scalability

### Visual Layout
High-level technology stack diagram showing React/Next.js PWA, FastAPI/Firebase backend, Gemini AI, and IndexedDB offline cache.

### Slide Content
* **Frontend:** Responsive React PWA, Tailwind CSS, Lucide icons, Web Speech API.
* **Backend:** Python FastAPI REST API with SQLite / PostgreSQL, JWT authentication, and RBAC.
* **Offline-First:** IndexedDB local persistence + Service Worker background sync for low-connectivity rural belts.
* **AI Engine:** Google Gemini API for vision triage and Kannada-to-structured clinical extraction.
* **Telephony Simulation:** Interactive Missed-Call & IVR simulator for basic feature-phone access.

### Speaker Script (35 Seconds)
> *"Under the hood, Pashu Pulse is built for Indian rural conditions. It is offline-first: farmers can log symptoms and record medicine doses even without 4G signal; it syncs automatically when signal returns. The backend uses clean REST APIs with role-based JWT security, and the AI layer is decoupled so the platform never fails even if the AI provider experiences downtime."*

## Slide 12: Business Model & Sustainability

### Visual Layout
Four revenue stream pillars with sustainable unit economics and B2B/B2G partnerships.

### Slide Content

4. **Government Biosurveillance API (B2G):** Anonymized early warning data feeds for the Department of Animal Husbandry and National Animal Disease Control Programme (NADCP).

### Speaker Script (40 Seconds)
> *"Our business model is built for rural viability. Farmers access basic reporting and passports for free, with affordable tele-triage fees.
